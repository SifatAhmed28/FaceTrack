Phase 1

Two-Stage Transfer Learning Strategy

Stage 1: Frozen Feature Extraction (Warmup)

The MobileNetV2 backbone is initialized with pre-trained ImageNet-1k weights. All 155 convolutional base layers are frozen to preserve learned low-level edge and texture representations.

  • Head: GlobalAveragePooling2D → Dense(128, ReLU) → Dropout(0.4) → Dense(4, Softmax)
  • Optimizer: Adam with learning rate $\eta = 1 \times 10^{-3}$
  • Loss Function: Categorical Cross-Entropy with Label Smoothing (0.05)
  • Epochs: 10 warmup epochs until classification head convergence

Stage 2: Deep Fine-Tuning

The top 30 layers of MobileNetV2 are unfrozen, allowing high-level convolutional kernels to adapt specifically to human skin sebum shine, epidermal texture, and inflammatory lesions.

  • Unfrozen Depth: Last 30 layers (inverted bottleneck blocks 14 through 16)
  • Optimizer: Adam with a reduced learning rate $\eta = 1 \times 10^{-5}$
  • Learning Rate Schedule: Cosine Annealing with warm restarts
  • Regularization: EarlyStopping (patience=5) & ModelCheckpoint on val_loss
Phase 2

Dataset Curation & Real-Time Augmentation

Diagnostic Dataset Partitioning

Trained across 3,000+ validated clinical facial diagnostic images divided into 4 mutually exclusive skin type classes and 4 lesion concern categories.

  • Skin Type Classes: Combination (T-zone), Dry, Normal, Oily
  • Lesion Severity Classes: Normal (Clear), Mild, Moderate, High Concern
  • Split Ratio: 70% Training, 15% Validation, 15% Holdout Test set
  • Resolution: Unified crop at 224 × 224 px RGB normalized to $[-1, 1]$

Real-Time Image Augmentation Pipeline

To ensure robust generalization across diverse smartphone cameras, camera sensors, and lighting conditions:

  • Horizontal Reflection: Random horizontal flip ($p=0.5$)
  • Affine Transformation: Random rotation ($\pm 15^\circ$) & zoom ($\pm 10\%$)
  • Photometric Jitter: Brightness adjustment ($\pm 15\%$) & contrast shifts
  • Face Alignment: YuNet bounding box expansion with $10\%$ protective margin

Phase 5

7-Dimensional Lifestyle Vector Formulation

Profile Embedding Equation:

To bridge the gap between static computer vision and real-world dermatological wellness, predictions are fused into a 7-dimensional user vector $\vec{u} \in \mathbb{R}^7$:

$\vec{u} = \Big[ \, u_{\text{oily}}, \; u_{\text{dry}}, \; u_{\text{normal}}, \; u_{\text{blemish}}, \; 0.5, \; 0.5, \; 0.5 \, \Big]$

$u_{\text{oily}} = P(\text{oily}) + 0.5 \cdot P(\text{combination}) + \mathbb{I}(\text{stress} > 7) \times 0.10$
$u_{\text{dry}} = P(\text{dry}) + 0.5 \cdot P(\text{combination}) + \mathbb{I}(\text{sleep} < 6) \times 0.15 + \mathbb{I}(\text{water} < 1.5) \times 0.15$
$u_{\text{blemish}} = P(\text{high}) \times 1.0 + P(\text{moderate}) \times 0.6 + P(\text{mild}) \times 0.3 + \mathbb{I}(\text{sleep} < 6) \times 0.10 + \mathbb{I}(\text{stress} > 7) \times 0.20$

The resulting normalized vector $\hat{u} = \frac{\vec{u}}{\|\vec{u}\|_2}$ is queried against the precomputed product catalog matrices using cosine similarity: $\text{score}_i = \frac{\vec{p}_i \cdot \vec{u}}{\|\vec{p}_i\| \|\vec{u}\|}$ in under 2 milliseconds.
